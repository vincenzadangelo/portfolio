How I work

From understanding to optimisation.

I do not start with the screen. I start by understanding the problem and the business.

I design, validate with real people and do not consider a solution finished until I understand what works. To me, design is a cycle, not a one-off delivery.

01

Research

Listen, observe and gather context before making decisions.

02

Understand the business

Align design with real goals, processes and constraints.

03

Design

Translate the problem into flows, structure, content and interface.

04

Validate

Test with real people before investing in development.

05

Iterate

Refine based on what we learn; the first attempt is never the last.

06

Measure

Use data to verify whether the solution solved the problem.

07

Optimise

Continuously refine the experience and the product.

AskMe → Assisted Shopping

AskMe was an internal Desigual tool. Research helped us understand how it was used in store, identify friction points and turn the findings into the product’s next evolution: Assisted Shopping.

ContextA tool used by store teams to support customer service.
ResearchInterviews, observations, device comparison, pain points and gain points.
SynthesisJourney mapping, clustering of friction points, ideas, needs and prioritised opportunities.
EvolutionFrom the analysis of AskMe to the patterns and components of Assisted Shopping.

Understanding what worked and what slowed people down

The study combined positive feedback, operational issues and a direct comparison between tablet and mobile.

AskMe · complete UX studyResearch, journey mapping, device comparison, friction points, improvements and ideas in one view.

Technical frictionWi-Fi, passwords and expired sessions could interrupt tasks.
Incomplete trackingDelivery, returns and status information needed greater clarity.

The designed journey

The journey connected every touchpoint and helped us design a coherent experience, not just an interface.

01 · Discovery

Find and understand

The website, search engines, social media and recommendations must clearly explain what we do and why it is different.

02 · First contact

Reduce uncertainty

Email, forms, WhatsApp or phone calls capture the initial idea and make it easy for customers to share context and images.

03 · Consultation

Help customers visualise

References, materials, dimensions and examples turn an abstract idea into a possible solution.

04 · Quote

Make the proposal tangible

A quote does not end the conversation: it structures options, decisions and expectations.

05 · Follow-up

Support the decision process

Answering questions and maintaining continuity during a decision that is often shared and takes time.

06 · Confirmation

Begin the service

Terms, payments, timelines and personalisation mark the beginning of the artisanal process.

07 · Production and delivery

Maintain trust

Coordination, progress updates, logistics and installation all contribute to perceived value.

08 · After-sales

Close the journey well

Follow-up, care guidance and issue resolution extend the experience beyond delivery.
